**NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)**.

Depending on the dataset or workflow, this may include comet identification information,

- JPL Solar System Dynamics: https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/

## 2. JPL Horizons

Hoshinotori-related workflows may use **JPL Horizons** for high-precision ephemeris or
apparent-position data.

- Service: https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/
- API documentation: https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/horizons.html

Horizons-derived values should be identified as NASA/JPL-derived where applicable.

Hoshinotori does not claim ownership of the underlying Horizons output or JPL-generated
astronomical values.
